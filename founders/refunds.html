Refund Policy

Last updated 28 November 2026.

If the pass is not for you, you can have your money back. Here is how.

1. Within 14 days: a full refund, no questions asked

Ask within 14 days of buying and we refund the full $49. You do not need to give a reason, and changing your mind is reason enough.

2. After 14 days

Refunds are at our discretion, and we look at each request fairly. We are most likely to say yes when something we promised has not worked for you.

3. If we fail to deliver

If something the pass promises does not reach you, and we cannot put it right within 30 days of your telling us, we refund you in full, whenever that happens.

4. How to ask

  1. Email hello@kairosmanor.com from the address you bought with, or include your receipt number.
  2. Say "refund". Nothing else is needed within the first 14 days.
  3. We confirm within 3 business days and send the refund through Stripe to the card you paid with. Your bank usually shows it within 5 to 10 business days.

5. After a refund

  • Your pass ends, and your name comes off the Founders' wall.
  • Your GOLEM records stay yours, in your browser, and you may keep any copy of the Kairos pack you made.

6. Please ask us first

If something is wrong, write to us before you dispute the charge with your bank. A refund from us is quicker for you than a chargeback.

7. Your other rights

This policy adds to any rights the law gives you; it does not take any away.
