Questions

Is Kairos a person?

No. Kairos is an AI butler, and he says so. Inside GOLEM he is written lines and notes built from your records; in your own Claude, he is the Kairos pack. He helps with goals, routines and the next action. He gives no medical, legal or financial advice, and steers you to a professional when that is what you need.

Yes. The house is free to walk as it stands, while it is built. The Founders' pass adds GOLEM: Founders' Edition, the Kairos pack and your place on the wall.

No. Your GOLEM, your copy of the Kairos pack and your name on the wall stay yours for good.

On your device. GOLEM keeps your goals, orders, books and reviews in your own browser and sends them nowhere; export a copy whenever you like. The Kairos pack runs in your own Claude account, under its own terms; we never see those chats. The privacy policy says exactly what we keep.

Yes, in the browser. GOLEM has a tab bar made for the thumb.

Can I change my mind?

Yes. A full refund within 14 days of buying, no questions asked. After that, we look at each request fairly. See the refund policy.
