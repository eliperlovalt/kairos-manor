Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 November 2026.

What we know about you, and what we never see. The short version: very little, and nothing of what you write in GOLEM.

1. Who we are

Kairos Manor, the trading name of Elijah Shaiova-Perlov, a sole proprietor in New York State, United States. We are responsible for the information described here. Contact: hello@kairosmanor.com.

2. What we collect

  • When you buy: your name and email address, the date, the amount and the receipt number. Stripe sends us these so we can give you a receipt, deliver what you bought, answer you, and keep the records the tax law requires.
  • The Founders' wall, if you choose: your first name and the initial of your surname, as you give them. Leave them out and nothing is shown. The wall is public, and the Estate's source, names included, is kept in a public record of its history: if you ask, we take your name down from the live site, but earlier copies of that history may keep it.
  • The waitlist, if you join it: your email address and the times you joined. We use them only to email you once, when the hundred places open, and for nothing else. To slow floods of sign-ups, the waitlist also keeps a scrambled form of your internet address, with the date, for ten minutes, then forgets it.
  • When you write to us: your message and your email address, so we can reply.

3. What Stripe handles, not us

Your card details go to Stripe, our payment processor, and never to us. Stripe processes them under its own privacy policy.

4. What we never collect

  • Your GOLEM records. Your goals, orders, notes, books and reviews stay in your own browser, on your device. They never reach us, and we cannot see them.
  • Your chats with Kairos in Claude. The Kairos pack runs in your own Claude account, under Anthropic's privacy terms. We never see those chats.

5. No selling, no ads, no trackers

We do not sell or rent your information, or share it for advertising. Our pages carry no ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies of our own.

6. Services that serve our pages

Like any website, our pages are delivered by other services, which see technical details such as your IP address and browser when they do so, under their own privacy policies. Today these are:

  • GitHub Pages, which hosts kairosmanor.com, the Estate and these pages;
  • Cloudflare, which receives the waitlist form and stores the waitlist for us, and passes email sent to hello@kairosmanor.com on to our inbox;
  • Google Fonts, which supplies the typefaces;
  • jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net), which supplies the code library that draws the house in your browser.

GOLEM: Founders' Edition is a file you keep on your own device. It fetches nothing but the typefaces from Google Fonts, and works without them. The Kairos pack runs in your own Claude account, under Anthropic's own terms.

We will update this list if it changes.

7. How long we keep it

  • Purchase records: as long as tax and accounting law requires.
  • The waitlist: until the places open and we have emailed you, or until you ask us to delete your entry, whichever comes first.
  • Your name on the wall: as long as the wall stands, or until you ask us to remove it.
  • Emails with you: as long as they are useful for helping you, then deleted.

8. Your choices

Write to hello@kairosmanor.com to see, correct or delete what we hold about you, to leave the waitlist, or to take your name off the wall. We reply within 30 days. We can delete everything except the purchase records the law requires us to keep; those we keep only as long as the law requires. Your GOLEM records are already in your hands: delete them with GOLEM's own Reset, or by clearing your browser's data.

9. Children

The pass is sold only to adults 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn that we have, we delete it and refund any purchase. A parent or guardian who believes we hold their child's information can write to us and we will delete it.

10. Keeping it safe

We keep as little as we can, and we keep card details nowhere. What we hold is kept in accounts protected by strong passwords and two-step sign-in.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is dated at the top. If a change matters to you, we will email you first.

12. Contact

Questions about your privacy: hello@kairosmanor.com.
